# Development Team

## Overview

The E-RANK project team, operating as **PMP Solutions**, consists of final-year ICT students at Sol Plaatje University. The team combines academic knowledge with practical application, developing a digital taxi rank management system while building competencies in mobile development, backend engineering, database design, and Technopreneurship.

The team is committed to delivering a functional, scalable, and user-centred system that addresses real-world challenges in the South African taxi industry.

## Team Philosophy

The E-RANK team is composed of emerging ICT professionals who are actively building their technical expertise through this project. While still developing into industry-ready practitioners, the team is confident in its ability to learn, adapt, and deliver a working system.

**Principles:**
- Continuous learning and skill development
- Collaborative problem-solving
- Transparent communication
- Commitment to quality and deadlines
